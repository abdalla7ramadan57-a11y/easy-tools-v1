AI Switcher Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 10, 2026

AI Switcher does not operate a backend service and does not collect, sell, rent, or transmit users' chat content or personal data to servers controlled by the extension developer.

Overview

AI Switcher is a Chrome extension that helps users switch between AI websites, including ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, DeepSeek, Perplexity, Manus, and custom AI websites added by the user. It can prepare and transfer conversation context between supported AI websites only when the user explicitly requests a transfer.

Transfer options may include My Prompts, Compact, Recent, and Full Chat modes. The amount and type of content processed depends on the mode selected by the user.

Data Processing

Website and chat content is processed only when the user explicitly initiates a transfer. Processing occurs locally in the user's browser for the purpose of preparing the requested context and moving it to the AI website selected by the user.

Depending on the selected mode, AI Switcher may process prompts or portions of the visible conversation. Full Chat mode may process visible user messages, assistant responses, code blocks, and visible attachment or file references. The extension does not process this content in the background for analytics, advertising profiles, or unrelated purposes.

Data Collection

AI Switcher has no backend server. The developer does not collect or receive users' conversations through the extension, does not sell or rent personal data, does not build advertising profiles, and does not track browsing behavior for analytics.

Local Storage

The extension uses browser local storage to save settings and data needed for its features. This may include:

  • User preferences and selected modes;
  • User-added custom AI website URLs;
  • Usage ordering used to arrange AI website options;
  • Promotional banner configuration; and
  • Temporary transfer state or prepared conversation context.

This information remains in the user's browser unless the user, the browser, or the extension removes it. Users can remove locally stored extension data by clearing the extension's site data or uninstalling the extension.

Clipboard Access

When direct insertion into another AI website is unavailable, AI Switcher may write the context prepared at the user's request to the clipboard as a fallback. The user can then paste that content into the selected service. Clipboard access is used to support the transfer initiated by the user.

Website Content

To perform a user-requested transfer, AI Switcher may read relevant visible content from the active supported AI website and may insert prepared context into the destination website. Website or chat content is accessed and processed only when the user explicitly initiates the transfer. The extension does not continuously monitor chat content or general browsing activity.

Third-Party AI Services

AI Switcher helps users interact with third-party AI websites but does not operate or control those services. When users submit, paste, or transfer content to ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, DeepSeek, Perplexity, Manus, a custom AI website, or another third-party service, that service's own privacy policy and terms apply. Users should review those policies before submitting content.

Advertising / Promotional Banners

The extension may display bundled rotating promotional or advertising banners. These banners and their configuration are packaged with the extension or stored locally. They do not track users, monitor browsing behavior, or build advertising profiles.

Remote Code

AI Switcher does not execute remotely hosted JavaScript or WebAssembly. Executable extension code is packaged with the Chrome extension. Third-party AI websites run their own code independently under their respective policies and are not part of the extension's executable code.

Data Sharing

The extension developer does not receive or share users' chat content or personal data through AI Switcher. Content is provided to a third-party AI service only as part of the action the user explicitly initiates, such as inserting or pasting prepared context into the destination website. Any resulting transmission is between the user's browser and the third-party service selected by the user.

Data Retention

The developer does not retain users' chat content or personal data on developer-controlled servers because AI Switcher does not operate a backend service. Preferences and other local data may remain in browser storage until they are replaced, cleared by the user or browser, or removed when the extension is uninstalled. Temporary transfer context is retained locally only as needed to complete or recover the user-requested transfer and may be overwritten or cleared by later extension activity.

Children's Privacy

AI Switcher is not directed to children under 13, and the developer does not knowingly collect personal information from children. Because the extension does not operate a backend service, it does not maintain user accounts or knowingly receive children's personal data.

Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated to reflect changes to the extension, legal requirements, or privacy practices. Any revision will be posted on this page with an updated date. Continued use of the extension after a revision means the revised policy will apply to future use.
